Why small businesses fail

Thin margins

Many owners work full time and still keep almost nothing, because prices and costs have drifted apart.

Hidden waste

Spoilage, idle hours and unused stock quietly drain cash without ever showing up as a single big problem.

No financial visibility

Without a clear view of the numbers, owners decide by instinct and notice trouble only when cash runs out.

Services

How it works

Four phases, and at each one the owner receives something concrete.

Phase 1

Diagnose

We record a baseline from your P&L and talk through the business with you.

You receive: a baseline snapshot.

Phase 2

Analyse

We find where margin is leaking and what is causing it.

You receive: your profit leak map.

Phase 3

Redesign

We build options, size their impact and rank them with you.

You receive: prioritised recommendations.

Phase 4

Implement and track

We help you act, then track the same metrics for 3 months.

You receive: an Excel dashboard and a results report.
